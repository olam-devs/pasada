## Free stock photos

All are free for commercial use without attribution under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) or the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Credits are kept here as a courtesy.
None is watermarked or editorial-only, and no caption identifies anyone's health status.
Resized to 1800px; colours unchanged.

**Consent.** These licences cover the photographer's copyright only; they come without
model releases (consent from the people shown). The Pexels licence also forbids showing
identifiable people "in a bad light". Next to HIV services, a recognisable face can suggest
that person has HIV, so every programme tied to HIV status (testing, ART, PMTCT, home-based
care, psychosocial support, OVC) uses either PASADA's own photo (cleared by PASADA management)
or a stock photo **without recognisable faces**. The remaining
photos with faces show health workers, training or general community health; replace them
with PASADA's own photos (taken with written consent) as soon as possible.
